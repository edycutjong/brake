Brake · system architecture

The agent can stop a payment. It can't make one.

Finance pre-signs every line twice: an EIP-3009 payment and its cancel, sharing one nonce. The agent reads vendor email, and its only power over money is to fire the cancel before payday. Its wallet holds 0 tUSD8 and no allowance.

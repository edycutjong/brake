Pre-signed stop-payments for stablecoin payment runs · v1.0 · 2026-11-16
Brake lets finance sign each vendor payment together with its own cancellation. A monitor agent reads the inbox and can stop a line before payday, but it holds no funds and cannot pay. Everything you will see ran on a fork of Aevum testnet; the live run waits on faucet funding.
L-01 Business email compromise, 2024
$2,770,151,146

in reported losses across 21,442 complaints to the FBI’s IC3. On a stablecoin chain, a paid line is final.

Source: FBI IC3 2024 Internet Crime Report, complaint and loss tables

The attack is mundane: a vendor's mailbox says the bank details changed. Card and wire rails have recall windows; a stablecoin transfer does not. That is the gap Brake closes.
L-02 The problem

Signed Monday. Wrong by Tuesday.

Today, stopping it needs the key holder online.

MondayRina, the AP clerk, signs four vendor lines.
Tuesday“Acme” emails new bank details.
PaydayThe line pays wherever it points.
Rina is our persona: the sole AP clerk. She approves on Monday, the fraud arrives Tuesday, and she is in meetings. Today her choices are to stay online with the key, or to hand a bot a key that can pay.
L-03 The idea

Sign the cancel with the payment.

Payment · EIP-3009transferWithAuthorization
valid from payday
to 0xFe83…a5De · 0.500
Cancellation · same noncecancelAuthorization
held for the agent
any caller may submitStop
Finance signs two EIP-712 messages per line in one sitting. tUSD8 accepts the signed cancel from any caller, which we proved on a fork with seven tests. So the agent that carries it needs no funds and no spending key.
L-04 How it works

Three days, one run.

Monday binds the agent's identity, a stop budget and an expiry. Tuesday the model reads every input as untrusted data and the contract submits the cancel with a reason and evidence hash. On payday the stopped line's replay reverts with the token's own error.

L-06 Least privilege

An agent with no wallet.

It may

  • allow a line
  • hold a line
  • stop ≤ maxStops

It cannot

  • pay anyone
  • change a payee
  • hold funds

0 tUSD8 and no allowance, checked on /verify · revocable at once.

A hijacked agent can delay a run: cancel up to maxStops lines and hold others. Each stop is re-issuable and a hold is released by one payer allow. It can never create a payment or redirect one.

L-09 Honest by design · README

What it cannot do.

R-1Brake prevents only what it detects.
R-2Brake acts only on what arrives before payday.
R-3It cannot claw funds back.
These are quoted from the README. A wrong stop costs a late payment. Had the attacker stayed silent, line 3 would have paid. If the payment lands first, Brake reports TOO LATE and opens a recovery ticket.
